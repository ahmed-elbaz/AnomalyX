Title: Promotion Underperformance Investigation Guide

Description:
Promotion underperformance occurs when a promotion is active but demand performs materially worse than expected promotional behavior.

Typical Indicators:
- Promotion is active.
- Demand is below historical promotional performance.
- Revenue may remain weak despite the promotion.
- Previous promotions may have generated stronger demand.

Possible Causes:
- Promotion is not attractive to customers.
- Discount level is insufficient.
- Inventory constraints.
- Competitor activity.
- Product demand has weakened.

Recommended Investigation:
- Compare the current promotion with previous promotion days.
- Compare current demand with historical promotional demand.
- Check inventory availability.
- Review price and discount behavior.
- Compare with similar products.

Recommended Actions:
- Review promotion effectiveness.
- Review discount strategy.
- Resolve inventory constraints if present.
- Investigate external market factors if internal indicators appear normal.

Important:
Promotion activation alone does not prove promotion underperformance. Performance should be compared with relevant historical promotional behavior.
