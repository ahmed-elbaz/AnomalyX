Title: Demand Spike Investigation Guide

Description:
A demand spike occurs when product sales rise significantly above the product's normal historical level.

Typical Indicators:
- Units sold are significantly above historical levels.
- Demand ratio is substantially above 1.
- Revenue may increase significantly.
- Similar products may continue performing normally.

Possible Causes:
- Successful promotion.
- Sudden increase in customer demand.
- Seasonal or external event.
- Price reduction.
- Unusual one-time purchase activity.

Recommended Investigation:
- Compare current demand with historical demand.
- Check whether a promotion is active.
- Review recent price changes.
- Check inventory levels to determine whether stock can support increased demand.
- Compare with similar products.

Recommended Actions:
- Ensure sufficient inventory is available.
- Review replenishment requirements.
- Determine whether the increase is temporary or sustained.
- Investigate promotion or pricing effects when applicable.

Important:
Do not assume the reason for increased demand without supporting evidence.
