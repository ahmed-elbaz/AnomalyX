Title: Price Increase Investigation Guide

Description:
A price increase anomaly occurs when a product's current price is significantly above its normal historical price.

Typical Indicators:
- Price ratio is significantly above 1.
- Current price is above historical levels.
- Demand may decline following the price increase.
- Revenue may increase or decrease depending on customer response.

Possible Causes:
- Intentional pricing change.
- Cost adjustment.
- Pricing configuration issue.
- End of a discount or promotional period.

Recommended Investigation:
- Compare current price with historical price.
- Check demand response after the price change.
- Compare price behavior with similar products.
- Check whether a promotion or discount recently ended.

Recommended Actions:
- Validate that the price change was intentional.
- Review demand impact.
- Correct pricing configuration if the increase was unintended.
- Review pricing strategy if demand deteriorates significantly.

Important:
A price increase can be detected from the data, but the business reason behind the change requires supporting evidence.
