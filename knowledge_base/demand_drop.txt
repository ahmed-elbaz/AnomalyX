Title: Demand Drop Investigation Guide

Description:
A demand drop occurs when product sales fall significantly below the product's normal historical level.

Typical Indicators:
- Units sold are significantly below historical levels.
- Demand ratio is substantially below 1.
- Revenue may decline with demand.
- Price and inventory may remain normal.
- Similar products may continue performing normally.

Possible Causes:
- Reduced customer demand.
- Competitor activity.
- Product-specific issue.
- Price increase.
- Insufficient inventory.
- End of a previous promotional effect.

Recommended Investigation:
- Compare current demand with historical demand.
- Check recent price changes.
- Check inventory availability.
- Check whether a promotion is active.
- Compare performance with similar products.

Recommended Actions:
- Investigate product-specific demand drivers.
- Review pricing if a price change occurred.
- Review inventory if availability is constrained.
- Review market or competitor factors if internal indicators appear normal.

Important:
Do not assign a specific cause unless supporting evidence is available.
