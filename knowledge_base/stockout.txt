Title: Stockout Investigation Guide

Description:
A stockout occurs when product inventory reaches zero and the product cannot satisfy customer demand.

Typical Indicators:
- Inventory is zero or extremely low.
- Units sold may fall sharply or reach zero.
- Revenue may fall sharply or reach zero.
- Similar products may continue performing normally.

Possible Causes:
- Replenishment delay.
- Supplier delivery issue.
- Inventory planning or forecasting error.
- Unexpected demand increase before the stockout.
- Replenishment quantity was insufficient.

Recommended Investigation:
- Check inventory levels before the stockout.
- Review recent demand trends.
- Check whether a demand spike occurred before inventory reached zero.
- Review replenishment and supplier delivery information if available.
- Compare with similar products to determine whether the issue is product-specific.

Recommended Actions:
- Replenish inventory if stock is unavailable.
- Review reorder thresholds.
- Adjust demand forecasts if demand was underestimated.
- Investigate supplier or replenishment delays where applicable.

Important:
Do not conclude that a specific cause occurred unless supporting evidence is available.
